Virginia Tech Hockey returns to the ice for the first time in seven months as the Hokies begin their 2026-27 season this weekend as they travel down south to take on the North Carolina State Icepack and UNC Tar Heels. After a season that brought fruits of an ACCHL Championship and another ACHA Southeast Regionals appearance, the program looks to continue the success into the new college hockey calendar year. 

Looking at what this season brings, Virginia Tech will go head-to-head with some of the best D1 and D2 programs on the eastern coast. The first half of the schedule sees Tech at home for 6 games, as opposed to their tough 16 road games. Four of those road games however are at a natural site as the Hokies will travel to Lake Placid and play Lindenwood, Sienna, and Weber State as part of the ACHA’s Fall Faceoff Invitational and later on return to Charlotte where they will face off against South Carolina at Truist Field. 

As opposed to a travel heavy first half, the new year brings five straight home games to close out the regular season. Tech does not travel until the second weekend in February as they will defend their ACCHL championship in Winston-Salem during the league’s annual tournament.

## Key Matchups: 

One of the key matchups for Tech will come in their season opener against North Carolina State in Raleigh. Last year, the Icepack dominated the Hokies 5-1 in Vinton, which was also the first game of the season, leaving a vengeful Tech squad looking to upset State on their home ice this Friday Night. Other early season highlights include Tech traveling to Lynchburg to face off against both of Liberty’s division one and two squads.

In the ACCHL, the premier division was refined this past offseason with the loss of Penn State to the TSCHL as the league promoted 2025-26 Elite Champions St. Joseph’s University to a stacked lineup of M2 squads. The Hokies will face UNCW, High Point, Wake Forest, West Virginia and Maryland during the regular season, and the remaining three teams (Rowan, St. Joes, and Delaware) in Springfield, Virginia during the annual ACCHL Fall Classic. 

## Outlook:

While anything can happen, the Admiral Cup Champs will be up for the challenge as they look to defend their championship and return to Regionals this season. With that in mind, we will see how battle tested the Hokies are this weekend as they face NC State and North Carolina, two opponents who with a win or weekend sweep, could instantly boost the team’s confidence heading into Lake Placid and beyond. 

Stay tuned all season long as the Hokies begin their 2026-27 campaign.
