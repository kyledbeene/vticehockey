College hockey is officially back as the Virginia Tech Hokies begin their 2026-27 season on the road this weekend with rival matchups against NC State and the University of North Carolina.

The Hokies come into the new season as reigning ACCHL Admiral Cup Champions, a label the program has not had on itself in nearly a decade. That said, last year’s result of another first round exit at regionals as well the departure of the program’s all-time leading scorer Gavin Peduzzi, poses challenging perspectives for how the team will fare this upcoming season starting on Friday Night against NC State.

In what has become an annual tradition for Tech and State to play on opening night, the Hokies have not seen success against the Icepack in sometime, dropping their last three meetings by a combined score of 13-5 all at home. This time the Hokies will hit the road, something they have not done to open a season since ___ to play the Icepack, where they last defeated State in 2025.

There will be new faces around for both programs, in prominent positions. For NC State, former Head Coach Tim Healy stepped down in May after nine seasons (six as coach) with the team. He will be succeeded by new Head Coach Nagib Ward who brings professional hockey experience to the role. On the other hand, Virginia Tech with the departure of Gavin Peduzzi will lean on key upperclassman Kam Khazai, JH Lages, and Tucker Forrest, while implementing several new faces to the lineup in what is considered to be a tough first leg of the season.

Saturday’s game against North Carolina is no walk in the park either for Virginia Tech. Although the Hokies have handled the Tar Heels as of late, including a sweep at home last season, there should be no doubt this game will be competitive for all 60 minutes. UNC already has two games under their belt with back-to-back shutout wins last weekend against UNC Charlotte. Both the Hokies and Tar Heels play away games on Friday Night before facing off Saturday Night. 

## Keys to Victory

# North Carolina State:

The key here is simple - Get on the board early and let your defense guide you to victory. The atmosphere inside the arena will be loud as NC State will bring the energy from the opening puck drop. By scoring first and early, not only will the Hokies blow the air out of the building, but will force the Icepack to change their tactics offensively. The defense especially will need to be ready for everything, as stamina will play an important role on who comes out on top. Offensively, the Hokies will need to generate scoring chances outside of Kam Khazai. With their all-time scoring leader Peduzzi gone, Tech will need individuals to step up and create opportunities at the net instead of being reliant. 

# North Carolina

Similar to NC State, stamina will play a critical factor in how the Hokies perform Saturday against the Tar Heels. Coming off what most likely will be a grueling game Friday against the Icepack, preparation and energy in that 24 hour window will be important in how the game goes. Expect to see some possible new faces in the lineup and them making an impact on the game. 

## Where to Watch

You can catch Friday night’s season opener at NC State on ESPN+ starting at 8:30pm followed by Saturday’s road trip finale at North Carolina on YouTube with puck drop set for 10pm.
