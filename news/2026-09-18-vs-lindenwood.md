# Strong Start not Good Enough as Hokies Drop to Lindenwood in First Game of ACHA Fall Faceoff Invitational

A historic weekend got underway for the Virginia Tech Men’s Ice Hockey program earlier today, as the Hokies took to the ice for the first of three games against Lindenwood at the ACHA’s Fall Faceoff Invitational in Lake Placid, New York, site of the legendary 1980 “Miracle on Ice.”

After coming strong in the opening minutes, Tech would be unable to sustain its momentum as the reigning 2025 Division II National Champions would eventually find their rhythm and take control of the contest, cruising to a 6-3 victory in Friday’s opener. 

Senior Kam Khazai notched his first goal of the season five minutes into the contest to give the Hokies an early lead over the Lions. Freshman Michael McCabe would continue his hot start by tacking on another goal later in the period to give Tech a 2-0 lead after the first period. 

From there, Lindenwood found their stride and did not look back. The Lions scored three unanswered goals in the middle frame, as well suffocating the Hokies power play unit who went 1-for-8 on the man advantage throughout the game. The lone score coming off the stick of McCabe who now has a team-leading three goals so far in 2026. 

Lindenwood would add three more goals in the third period to finalize a 6-3 victory. Hokies Junior netminder Tucker Forrest went 46/52 in net, while the offense generated only 36 shots on goal. 

Virginia Tech will look to capitalize on a win tomorrow afternoon as they face Sienna for game two inside the 1980 Herb Brooks arena. Puck drop is set for 1:15pm live on FloHockey.
