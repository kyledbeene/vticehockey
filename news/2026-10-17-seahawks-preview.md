After a quick road trip down in Winston-Salem, the Virginia Tech Men’s Ice Hockey team return to home ice Friday and Saturday night as they host the UNCW Seahawks for two critical league games. 

The Hokies (4-5, 1-0) are coming off a sweep of division rival Wake Forest in which the team outscored the Demon Deacs 11-4. Moreover Saturday’s 5-3 win secured Virginia Tech three points in the ACCHL standings, slotting them ahead and tied for third overall in the early stages of the league’s season.  

UNC-Wilmington (7-3, 2-0) continued their strong start to the season as the Seahawks competed in the Stephen Russell Tournament in Morrisville, North Carolina, where they faced off against Texas A&M, St. Thomas (FL), and Delaware. A productive set of games overall of which the Seahawks took their first two before dropping to the Blue Hens in their last game. 

What to Expect this Weekend:
There is no love lost between these two programs, especially now in a year where the top spot of the league has never been more wide open. Look for a statement to be made early on Friday night by either team. The Hokies’ will need to be physical and create opportunities for their playmakers to capitalize. On the other hand, the Seahawks will try to match that with their own strength and remain vigilant on the offensive end in blistering Tech’s stamina on defense. 

Friday evening will also mark the introduction of Virginia Tech’s all-time scoring leader and former captain Gavin Peduzzi into the ACCHL Hall of Fame. Peduzzi (‘25) currently holds all but one scoring record with the Hokies in five seasons played with the program and led the team to several regional appearances as well its first ACCHL championship in over a decade. Congrats to Gavin on this tremendous accomplishment!

Where to Watch:
The Virginia Tech Hokies will host the UNCW Seahawks at the Lancelot Sports Complex for a two-game series. Puck drops on Friday at 7:30pm after the Hall of Fame induction ceremony and 6:30pm on Saturday. Both games will be available to watch on the Virginia Tech Hockey Network on Youtube.
