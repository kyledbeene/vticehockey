Add one Markdown file per article to this folder in GitHub.

Example front matter:
---
title: Hokies open the season with a win
category: GAME RECAP
author: Your Name
summary: A short summary shown on the news card.
date: 2026-09-12
---

Write the full article here. The first 170 characters become the fallback summary. The optional author field appears as a byline on the full article page.
